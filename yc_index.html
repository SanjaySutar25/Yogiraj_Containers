About Us

Precision Manufacturing for the Paint Industry

Yogiraj Containers Pvt. Ltd. is a dedicated B2B manufacturer of steel paint containers, operating from the Ankleshwar GIDC Industrial Estate in Bharuch, Gujarat. We specialize in producing Mild Steel (MS) Round and Galvanised Iron (GI) Square paint containers for India's leading paint companies.

Our manufacturing facility is equipped with modern machinery and adheres to strict quality standards at every stage — from raw material sourcing to finished product dispatch — ensuring consistent, reliable supply to our clients.
