Product 01

MS Round Paint Container

Our Mild Steel (MS) Round paint containers are manufactured from high-quality cold-rolled steel, offering superior strength and durability for the safe storage and transport of paints and coatings.

The seamless cylindrical body, precision-formed lid, and secure locking ring ensure a completely leak-proof seal — protecting your product from contamination and spillage throughout the supply chain.

Material: Mild Steel (MS) Shape: Round / Cylindrical Multiple Capacities Leak-Proof Seal BIS Compliant

Product 02

GI Square Paint Container

Our Galvanised Iron (GI) Square containers offer excellent corrosion resistance thanks to the hot-dip galvanising process — making them ideal for water-based and solvent-based paints that require a longer shelf life.

The square form factor allows for efficient packing and stacking during warehousing and logistics, maximising space utilisation and reducing freight costs for our B2B clients.

Material: Galvanised Iron (GI) Shape: Square Corrosion Resistant Stackable Design BIS Compliant
