About Us

An Indian Leader in Steel Drum Manufacturing

Metal Fass Inc. is an Indian manufacturer of standard and custom designed steel barrels and drums. Whether you pack hazardous chemicals or food products, our cold-rolled carbon steel barrels and drums provide superior safety and durability.

We manufacture a full range of new steel barrels and drums for all your shipping needs — built to Indian and International specifications, including BIS standards for selective products.

Accredited by BVQI, our certifications are a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality, environmental responsibility, and occupational health & safety.

✅ ISO 9001:2015 ✅ ISO 14001:2015 ✅ ISO 45001:2018 ✅ BIS Standards
